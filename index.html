      About this measure
      Attempts per game

      This week's rest edges

      Only the factors with real evidence behind them count (see below). Points are added to the team's expected margin.

      What actually matters

      How this is calculated
      Data
      Every regular-season game since 2022, refit each morning as new results come in.
      Effect
      Points added to or taken from a team's final margin when it has the factor and its opponent doesn't, after adjusting for home field and the other factors.
      Evidence
      "Real" means less than a 5% chance the effect is luck, "Likely" less than 10%, "No clear effect" anything weaker. Only Real and Likely factors count toward the rest edge.
      Vs. the spread
      Whether the effect still shows up after the betting line. If it doesn't, the sportsbooks already price it in.

      Flags per game: 2025 to 2026

      Grey dot is last season, colored dot is this season. Long lines are crews that changed the most.

      Crew tendencies, 2026

      Accepted penalties per game. Home lean is home-team flags minus away-team flags per game, so negative means the road team gets flagged more.

      Betting tendencies and last week's assignments

      Career against-the-spread records for the home team, notable over/under lean, and the game each crew worked last week.

      Touchdown luck: who's due to cool off or heat up

      Expected TDs give each player the league-average touchdown rate on his carries or catches. Big positive gaps tend to shrink, so sell high; big negative gaps are buy-low targets.

      Usage without payoff

      Players getting real volume (10+ carries and targets a game) but the fewest fantasy points per touch. The usage is sticky; the points often catch up.

      Sack magnets

      Share of dropbacks that end in a sack, for passers with 100+ attempts. High rates sink fantasy floors and stall drives.
