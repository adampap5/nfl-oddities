How the fatigue score works
Short week
+3 for four days of rest or fewer, +1 for five or six. Ten or more days, or a bye, is −2.
Distance
+1 for each full 1,000 miles from home.
Time zones
+1 for each zone beyond the first. An overseas game is +3.
Body clock
+2 when kickoff lands before 11:30 AM on the team's home clock.
Altitude
+1 for a visiting team in a stadium above 5,000 feet.

Touchdown luck: who's due to cool off or heat up

Expected TDs give each player the league-average touchdown rate on his carries or catches. Big positive gaps tend to shrink, so sell high; big negative gaps are buy-low targets.

Usage without payoff

Players getting real volume (10+ carries and targets a game) but the fewest fantasy points per touch. The usage is sticky; the points often catch up.

Sack magnets

Share of dropbacks that end in a sack, for passers with 100+ attempts. High rates sink fantasy floors and stall drives.
